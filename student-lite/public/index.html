How this works

Your bid is the fewest points you would accept to do a job. Jobs pay you.

Each class a wage is drawn at random for each job. If your bid is at or below it you are in the pool, and one person in the pool is picked. Whoever is picked is paid the drawn wage, not their bid.

So bid the lowest amount you would honestly accept. A higher bid cannot raise your pay. A job left blank means you are not in the market for it.

Today is a practice class: jobs are given at random to anyone in the class, and no points are paid today.

Earned points count toward participation. Spending points does not lower your grade.
