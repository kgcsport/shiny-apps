# Class Jobs and Participation Points

Status: student-facing draft. Replace every bracketed item before publication.

## The short version

Our class needs real work: explaining graphs, summarizing ideas, asking useful
questions, checking evidence, and helping the class make connections. You will
have opportunities to do these jobs and earn participation points.

During the first two weeks, we will practice the jobs. Practice jobs are
assigned by lottery: most students are drawn for two practice jobs and a few
for none, so whether and what you practice is a matter of chance. No points
are paid during this practice period. Practice work is paid at the end of the term, at
the average wage the same job earned in the other section of the course. You will be asked what you would need to be paid
for each job, but those early answers do not assign any jobs. You will receive
short feedback so that you know what each job requires before the market
begins.

When the market opens in week three, everyone first enters a bid for every job.

Beginning in week three:

1. You enter the minimum number of participation points you would accept to do
   each available job.
2. Before each class the app draws a wage for each job at random. The draw
   does not depend on anyone's bid.
3. Everyone whose bid is at or below that wage is in the running, and the job
   goes to students picked by lottery from that group. Everyone picked earns
   the drawn wage, which is at least what they asked for.
4. If nobody's bid is at or below the wage, the job may be offered to the class
   at that wage. Anyone can step in; otherwise it is not filled that day.
5. Completing the job earns the drawn wage. Partial and missing work follow
   the posted outcome rules.

Your individual bids are private. The drawn wage and assignments are shown
after bidding closes.

## What participation points are worth

Participation is 15% of the course grade. [TARGET] gross participation
points earn full credit for that part of the course.

Up to the full-credit threshold:

The app keeps two totals:

- **Gross points earned** determine participation credit.
- **Points on hand** can be spent on class perks.

Spending points does not take away participation credit you already earned. For
example, if you earn 10 points and spend 4, your participation record still
shows 10 gross points earned and your wallet shows 6 points on hand.

Points may be spent on [FINAL PERK MENU, PRICES, AND AVAILABILITY DATES]. The
complete menu and prices will be posted before the first bid. Required course
materials, ordinary help, accessibility accommodations, and normally released
solutions never require points.

## Cold calls and volunteering

Answering a question and working at the board can happen by cold call or by
volunteering, and both pay the same wage. That wage comes from the class's
bids and is fixed when the first draw of the day runs.

You are always welcome to contribute. You are paid for one voluntary
contribution per class. After that, further contributions that day are
recorded and count as participation in the discussion, but they are not paid.

The limit applies to payment, not to speaking or asking questions.

## Feedback

An observed job receives one outcome:

- **Success:** met the job criterion and was useful;
- **Try:** made a genuine attempt and met part of the criterion;
- **Fail:** responded, but the response did not yet perform the assigned job;
  or
- **Miss:** made no usable attempt.

An excused absence is recorded separately and is not a Miss. You may also
receive one or two short suggestions such as **Get to the point**, **Lead with
the claim**, **Show the mechanism**, **Nice summary**, or **Prepare more**.

## What you should bid

Enter the smallest wage at which you are genuinely willing to accept the job.
Your bid cannot change the wage. It only decides which wages you are in the
running for. Bid above your true minimum and you miss jobs you would have
wanted at that pay; bid below it and you may be picked for pay you find too
low. What other students bid does not change what you should enter.
